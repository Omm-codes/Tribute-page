Dr. Norman Borlaug

The man who saved a billion lives through agricultural innovation

About Dr. Norman Borlaug

Dr. Norman Borlaug was an American agronomist, humanitarian, and Nobel laureate. Known as "The Father of the Green Revolution," his work significantly increased agricultural productivity worldwide, saving millions from starvation. He dedicated his life to addressing global hunger and promoting peace through food security.

Key Milestones in His Life

1914

Born in Cresco, Iowa.

1933

Leaves his family farm to attend the University of Minnesota.

1944

Joins the Rockefeller Foundation's agricultural program in Mexico.

1970

Awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for combating world hunger.

1986

Establishes the World Food Prize.

2009

Passes away at the age of 95, leaving a legacy of hope and innovation.

Awards and Recognition

  • Nobel Peace Prize (1970)
  • Presidential Medal of Freedom (1977)
  • Congressional Gold Medal (2007)
  • Padma Vibhushan (India, 2006)
  • Founder of the World Food Prize
